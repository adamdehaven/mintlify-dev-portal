Your account dashboard is the central hub for everything related to your presence on the Developer Portal. From here you can update your profile, generate and rotate API keys, review your active subscriptions, monitor real-time usage metrics, manage billing details, and — if you're on a team plan — invite colleagues to collaborate. Every setting that affects how you authenticate, consume, and pay for APIs lives under your account.

## Accessing Account Settings

To open your account settings, click your **avatar or username** in the top-right corner of any portal page, then select **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu. You can also navigate directly to `/account` in the portal URL.

## Key Areas of the Dashboard

| Section | What you manage |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Display name, email address, notification preferences |
| **API Keys** | Create, rotate, and revoke authentication keys |
| **Subscriptions** | View active plans and subscribed APIs |
| **Usage** | Request counts, rate-limit headroom, quota consumption |
| **Billing** | Invoices, payment methods, plan upgrades |

## Profile Settings

Your profile controls the identity associated with your portal account. To update your profile:

1. Navigate to **Account Settings → Profile**.
2. Edit your display name, email address, or company name.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Changes to your email address trigger a confirmation link sent to the new address. The update takes effect only after you click that link.

## Account Security

Keeping your account secure protects both your API credentials and any sensitive usage data tied to your subscriptions.

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Account Settings → Security**.
2. Click **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.
4. Click **Update Password**.

You'll receive an email confirmation whenever your password changes. If you didn't initiate the change, contact support immediately.

### Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Settings">
    Navigate to **Account Settings → Security** and click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR Code">
    Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, or any TOTP-compatible app) and scan the QR code displayed on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the Code">
    Enter the six-digit code generated by your authenticator app to confirm pairing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Backup Codes">
    Copy and store the backup codes in a safe place. These codes let you regain access if you lose your authenticator device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Backup codes are shown only once. Store them securely — losing both your authenticator device and your backup codes will lock you out of your account and require identity verification through support.
</Warning>

## Inviting Team Members

If your account is on a **Pro** or **Enterprise** plan, you can invite colleagues to share access to your organization's API keys, subscriptions, and billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Navigate to **Account Settings → Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an Invitation">
    Enter your colleague's email address and choose their role — **Viewer**, **Developer**, or **Admin** — then click **Send Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await Acceptance">
    Your colleague receives an invitation email. Once they accept, they appear in your team member list with the role you assigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Team management is available on Pro and Enterprise plans only. If you're on the Free or Basic plan and need to collaborate, consider upgrading your subscription first.
</Note>
